THE CRIMSON HUNT VR  -  VR mod for "The Crimson Hunt" (Unity 6000.3.11, IL2CPP x64, HDRP, Game Creator 2)
=========================================================================================================

Version 0.1.9 (test build on Quest 3 VDXR).

CHANGES
-The task marker (hold R3) floats by the left hand too.

-Left hand HUD left / right: VR settings menu, or [UI] LeftHandHudShift (- = further left, + = right; past the hand it hangs on the right side of the hand).

-Subtitles: the whole speech box of the dialogue now goes to the left hand

-Ammo and the partner icon float to the right of the weapon in your hand ([UI] RightHandHud,

-Crosshair hidden in first person - the laser shows where you aim ([Aiming] HideCrosshair). 

-Fog and light: [Look] Fog = Game / Light / Off, FogDistance (how far you see in light fog),

-Brightness (-3 .. +3). All in the VR settings menu under "Fog and light".

-Option to swap RB (right grip) and LT ([Input] SwapRBAndLT, also in the VR settings menu).

INSTALL
1. Unzip folder of this zip over the game's folder.
2. Start Quest Link / Virtual Desktop / SteamVR first (it must be the OpenXR runtime), then the game.

HOW IT PLAYS
-
- First person (default): you are the character - look around freely, the game looks where you look
  (walking, turning, using). The weapon is in your right hand and fires where you point it (red laser).
  Right stick = snap turn. Left grip + right stick click = switch to the game's own camera and back.
- The game's own camera view, cutscenes, puzzles, fixed views: you stand where the game's camera is and can
  look around; the right stick moves the game camera; you still aim with the right controller.
- The game's menus and HUD are on a floating screen in front of you.

CONTROLS (the VR controllers are an Xbox controller for the game)
-
  Left stick ....... left stick (walk)
  Right stick ...... turn in first person (game camera view / menus: right stick)
   
   SwapRBAndLT on: right grip = LT, left trigger = RB)
   
  X + Y together ... Start (pause)
  
  Left grip + Y .... Back / View
  
  Hold A + left stick = d-pad (up / down / left / right); tap A = A
  
  B (or Escape) .... close a note you picked up
  
  Hold left stick click 3 s ... flashlight on / off
  
  Left grip + right stick click ... first person <-> the game's camera
  
  Both grips + right stick click ... adjust the weapon in your hand
  
  Both stick clicks  re-centre the view
  
  Y + left stick click (hold) ... VR settings menu
  
  Change any of it in [Controls].
